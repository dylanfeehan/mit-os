va 0 pte 0x21FCF45B pa 0x87F3D000 perm 0x5B

- for address 0, the number to the right of "pte" is the pte of the level 2 page table. this is the final page table, indexed by the lowest 9 bits of the 27 bits of the VA that index the page tables. whereas PT2 and PT2 PTEs contain PPN of n-1 page table, PT0 PPN is the PPN of the actual physical address. To get the PPN, shift pte >> (right) by 10 bits 
- 0x21FCF45B -> 0x21FCF4 (rids 8 bits), in binary: 0010 0001 1111 1100 1111 0100, drop last two bits : (see below)
- 0010 0001 1111 1100 1111 01 -> 1000 0111 1111 0011 1101 -> 87F3D, this indexes page -> 87F3D000 (to add the offset bitch now it indexes a char (byte)
